# Discussion Guide: Personal Budget Simulator

## Milestone 2 — Income
- Ask the student to explain their code around their inputs. 
  - Students should understand that the input function always returns a string and that to do math with the input, it should be converted to an int/foat. 
  - The student should also understand when to convert a string to a float/int.
- Ask the student to explain the calculation for the "gross_income". 
  - Why did they multiply hourly_wage, hours_per_week, and num_weeks together?

## Milestone 3 — Fixed Expenses
- Ask the students to explain their print statements and why they chose to accomplish the task that way. 
  - Did they use fstrings? 
  - Did they print multiple values offset with commas?
  - Did they concatenate everything together?
  - Did they use one print statement and newlines? 
- Ask the student to explain their code on how they rounded the values off when they printed them out.

## Milestone 4 — Variable Spending Estimate
- Ask the student to explain why their calculations for their discretionary_percent_of_income variable.
- Ask the student why he/she multiply by 100 here?

## Milestone 5 — Unexpected Expenses
- Ask the student to explain how they calculated the left-over amount.
- Walk me through "difference = leftover - adjusted_leftover". 

## Design & Judgment Calls (ask at least one)
- Where in this file did you reference variables across multiple milestones? 
- Discuss some of their submission comments where they discussed changes made from one submission to the next.
- What was the single hardest bug to track down in this lab, and how did you find it?
---

## Quick pass/fail signal for the TA
A student who understands their own code should be able to:
1. Point to any line and say what it computes and why it's needed.
2. Should be able to discuss design decisions.
